## 2. Step-by-Step Evaluator Walkthrough

### Stage 1: Initial Presentation (0 - 15s)
1. **First Impression:** The workstation boots in the refined **Opal Ceramic** light theme. The canvas mesh gradient and sub-pixel bordered bento plates immediately communicate luxury workstation ergonomics.
2. **Auto-Hydration:** Without any login prompt, the browser loads the active IndexedDB ledger from `masterSeed.json`, presenting real liquidity metrics ($24,850+).
3. **Fluid Motion:** The **Live Cash-Flow Particle Stream** dynamically moves particles from left (Inflows) across three middle distribution pillars (Needs, Wants, Savings) to right (Asset Vaults), reflecting exact transaction weights.

### Stage 2: Omnimodal Exploration (15 - 60s)
1. **Persona Swapping:** Click the persona dropdown in the top header and select **"Global Tech Freelancer"**. Confetti fires, audio haptics chime, and all figures recalibrate across USD, EUR, and PKR.
2. **Keyboard Control:** Press `⌘K` to open the Omnimodal Command Palette. Type a natural sentence (e.g., *"Dinner with team $85"*), or switch to Voice mode and speak.
3. **Voice Activation:** Speak *"Hey Aura"* into your microphone. The continuous Web Speech listener wakes the 3D Canvas audio frequency orb and confirms your liquidity position.

### Stage 3: Institutional Precision & Cultural Finance (60 - 120s)
1. **General Ledger & Trial Balance:** Navigate to **General Ledger**. View the journal entry creation desk. Notice how an unbalanced entry ($100 debit vs $90 credit) is strictly blocked by the balance sentinel. The live Trial Balance displays **$0.00 variance** across all 5 account classes.
2. **Precious Metals Bullion:** On the Dashboard, scroll to the **Commodities & Bullion Vault**. Toggle between **Grams**, **Troy Ounces**, and **South Asian Tolas (11.66g)**. Real-time spot rates recalculate holdings and unrealized P&L instantly.
3. **Multimodal Receipt Scan:** Click **Multimodal OCR Receipt Scan**. Upload or drag any receipt image. Gemini 2.0 Flash extracts line items, computes tax, and converts foreign currency into the base ledger account.

### Stage 4: Cryptographic Vault & Internationalization (120 - 180s)
1. **Audit-Grade 24H Statement PDF:** Click **24h Statement PDF** in the top hero banner. In less than 1.5 seconds, a client-side vector PDF is generated in memory featuring institutional typography, cryptographic SHA-256 integrity checksums, and audit trail tables.
2. **Encrypted Vault Backup:** In **Settings**, test the AES-GCM 256-bit vault backup. Enter a passphrase to generate an encrypted JSON bundle derived with PBKDF2.
3. **Universal Vernacular Translation:** Click the Language dropdown and select **اردو (Urdu)**. The entire interface transforms instantly into authentic Urdu vernacular terms (e.g., راشن راڈار, مصیبت کا لفافہ, کمیٹی) with full RTL spatial mirroring in under 50 milliseconds.
