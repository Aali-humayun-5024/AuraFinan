## 🎙️ Recording Best Practices & Quality Checklist
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record browser maximized at 1920 × 1080 (100% DPI scaling).
2. **Audio Narration:** Clear, steady delivery without background noise.
3. **Cursor Movement:** Smooth, deliberate navigation; use keyboard shortcuts (`⌘K`, `⌘M`, `Esc`) to highlight polish.
4. **Theme Presentation:** Toggle between Light and Dark mode during Scene 1 and Scene 7 to showcase responsive contrast.
5. **Output Format:** Render as a standard `.mp4` file encoded in H.264 / AAC audio under 100 MB for jury submission.
